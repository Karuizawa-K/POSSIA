FORGOT YOUR PASSWORD?

1. On the login page, select "Forgot password?".
2. Enter the email address bound to your account and select "Send code".
3. Enter the 6-digit one-time code from the email, plus your new password twice.
4. Sign in with your new password.

DETAILS
- The code expires in 10 minutes and can only be used once.
- Sending a new code replaces the previous one.
- After 5 incorrect attempts, request a new code.
- If you no longer have access to your bound email, ask your Admin to update
  the email on your account in Admin Settings.

Your Admin can also set a new password for you directly in Admin Settings.
Never share your password with anyone.
