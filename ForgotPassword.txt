FORGOT YOUR PASSWORD?

If you are the POSSIA owner and cannot sign in:

1. On the login page, select "Forgot owner password?"
2. Enter your owner username, the recovery code given to you by your system
   operator, and your new password twice.
3. Sign in with your new password.

Don't have a recovery code? Contact your system operator or support person.
They will verify your identity and give you a code securely. Never share your
password with anyone.

This option is only for the system owner. It changes the password, not who
owns the system.
