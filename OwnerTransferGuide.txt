ACCOUNT OWNERSHIP - CLIENT GUIDE

WHAT IS THE OWNER?
Your system has one "owner" account. The owner is one of your Admins.

- All Admins can manage user accounts.
- Only the owner can delete or demote another Admin.
- Only the owner can edit the owner account.
- The owner account cannot be deleted or demoted.


WHAT YOU CAN DO YOURSELF

Transfer ownership to another Admin
1. Sign in as the owner and open Admin Settings.
2. Choose the Admin who will become the new owner.
3. Enter your password and confirm.
4. The change happens right away. The previous owner stays an Admin but loses owner-only privileges.


PLEASE CONTACT YOUR TRUSTED OPERATOR FOR THE FOLLOWING

- First-time setup or after a system update:
  If Admin Settings says owner-only actions are unavailable, contact your trusted operator. They will fix this for you.

- The owner is unavailable, or the owner record is missing or broken:
  Contact your trusted operator for emergency recovery. They will give you a one-time recovery code and tell you what to do next.

- Any other technical change related to ownership.


KEEP IN MIND
- Only your trusted operator should hold the recovery code. Do not share it with other Admins.
- Each recovery code works only once. After a recovery, your operator will need to prepare a new one before it can be used again.
