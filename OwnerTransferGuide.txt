ACCOUNT OWNER

Your system has one owner account. The owner is also an Admin.

- Admins can manage user accounts.
- Only the owner can delete or remove Admin access from another Admin.
- The owner account cannot be deleted or removed as an Admin.

TRANSFER OWNERSHIP

1. Sign in as the owner and open Admin Settings.
2. Choose the Admin who will become the new owner.
3. Enter your password and confirm the change.

The transfer takes effect right away. The previous owner stays an Admin but
loses owner-only permissions.

NEED HELP?

Contact your trusted system operator if:
- Owner-only actions are unavailable in Admin Settings.
- The owner cannot sign in or the owner account is unavailable.
- You need to recover or change ownership.

Never share your password. Only provide a recovery code to the person
authorized by your organization to handle system recovery.
